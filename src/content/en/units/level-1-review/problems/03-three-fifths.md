What is $\frac{3}{5}$ of 40?

## Hint

Make 5 equal groups first.

## Hint 2

Each group has 8. Take 3 groups.

## Solution

Make 5 equal groups: $40 \div 5 = 8$. Take 3 of them: $3 \times 8 = 24$.

**Answer: (D) 24**
