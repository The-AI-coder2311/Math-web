What is the value of $125 \times 7 \times 8$?

## Hint

Which two numbers make a friendly product?

## Hint 2

$125 \times 8 = 1000$.

## Solution

Since $125 \times 8 = 1000$, the product is $1000 \times 7 = 7000$.

**Answer: (D) 7000**
