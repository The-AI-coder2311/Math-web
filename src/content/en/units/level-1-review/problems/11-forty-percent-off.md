A \$60 pair of shoes is on sale for 40% off. What is the sale price, in dollars?

## Hint

Find 40% of 60. Is that the price, or the discount?

## Hint 2

10% of 60 is 6, so 40% is 24.

## Solution

40% of 60 is 24, so the sale price is $60 - 24 = 36$ dollars.

**Answer: (D) 36**
