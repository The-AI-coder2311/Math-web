A rectangular poster is 6 feet wide and 9 feet tall. What is its area, in square feet?

## Hint

Area counts squares. How many rows are there, and how many squares are in each?

## Hint 2

Multiply the two sides.

## Solution

The area is $6 \times 9 = 54$ square feet.

**Answer: (C) 54**
