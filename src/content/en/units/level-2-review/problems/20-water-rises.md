A tank has a base that is 10 inches by 5 inches, and it's partly filled with water. A solid cube with sides of 5 inches is lowered in and is completely underwater, with no water spilling out. How many inches does the water rise?

## Hint

How much water does the cube push aside?

## Hint 2

It pushes aside 125 cubic inches, which spread over a base of 50 square inches.

## Solution

The cube's volume is 125 cubic inches. That water spreads over $10 \times 5 = 50$ square inches. So it rises $125 \div 50 = 2.5$ inches.

**Answer: (E) 2.5**
