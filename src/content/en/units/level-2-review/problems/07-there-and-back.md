A driver goes 72 miles at 36 miles per hour and returns the same way at 24 miles per hour. What is the average speed for the round trip, in miles per hour?

## Hint

Average speed is total distance divided by total time.

## Hint 2

It takes 2 hours out, and 3 hours back.

## Solution

It takes 2 hours out and 3 hours back. That's 144 miles in 5 hours, and $144 \div 5 = 28.8$.

**Answer: (A) 28.8**
