What is the sum of the inside angles of a pentagon, in degrees?

## Hint

How many triangles do you get by drawing lines from one corner?

## Hint 2

A pentagon splits into 3 triangles.

## Solution

A pentagon splits into $5 - 2 = 3$ triangles, so the sum is $3 \times 180 = 540$ degrees.

**Answer: (D) 540**
