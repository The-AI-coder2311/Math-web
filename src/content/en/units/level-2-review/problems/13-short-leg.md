A 30-60-90 triangle has a hypotenuse of 18. How long is its short leg?

## Hint

The triangle is half of an equilateral triangle.

## Hint 2

The hypotenuse is twice the short leg.

## Solution

The hypotenuse is twice the short leg, so the short leg is $18 \div 2 = 9$.

**Answer: (B) 9**
