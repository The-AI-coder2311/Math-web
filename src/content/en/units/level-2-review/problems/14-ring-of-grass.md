Two circles share the same center, with radii of 10 and 6. What is the area of the ring between them?

## Hint

Subtract the part you don't want.

## Hint 2

The areas are $100\pi$ and $36\pi$.

## Solution

Subtract the areas: $100\pi - 36\pi = 64\pi$.

**Answer: (E) $64\pi$**
