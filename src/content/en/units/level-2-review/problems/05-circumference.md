What is the circumference of a circle with a radius of 9?

## Hint

Use the formula for the distance around.

## Hint 2

The formula is $2 \pi r$.

## Solution

The circumference is $2 \times 9 \times \pi$, which is $18\pi$.

**Answer: (B) $18\pi$**
