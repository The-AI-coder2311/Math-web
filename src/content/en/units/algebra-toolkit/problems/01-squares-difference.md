What is the value of $53^2 - 47^2$?

## Hint

Use the difference of squares pattern, $a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$.

## Hint 2

$a + b = 100$ and $a - b = 6$.

## Solution

Use the pattern:

$$53^2 - 47^2 = (53 + 47)(53 - 47) = 100 \times 6 = 600$$

**Answer: (E) 600**
