What is $52^2 - 48^2$?

## Hint

Use $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$.

## Hint 2

Here, $a - b = 4$ and $a + b = 100$.

## Solution

$52^2 - 48^2 = (52 - 48)(52 + 48) = 4 \times 100 = 400$.

**Answer: (C) 400**
