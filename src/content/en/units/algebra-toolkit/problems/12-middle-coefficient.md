When $(x + 3)^2$ is multiplied out, what number goes in front of $x$?

## Hint

Write it as $(x + 3)(x + 3)$. How many times do you get $3x$?

## Hint 2

You get $3x$ twice.

## Solution

$(x + 3)(x + 3) = x^2 + 3x + 3x + 9 = x^2 + 6x + 9$. So the number is 6.

**Answer: (D) 6**
