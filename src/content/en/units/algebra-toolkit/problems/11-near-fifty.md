What is the value of $47 \times 53$?

## Hint

Both numbers are the same distance from a round number. Which one?

## Hint 2

They're 3 away from 50. So it's $50^2 - 3^2$.

## Solution

$47 \times 53 = (50 - 3)(50 + 3) = 50^2 - 3^2 = 2500 - 9 = 2491$.

**Answer: (B) 2491**
