Lena bikes 60 miles at 30 miles per hour. Then she bikes 60 more miles at 20 miles per hour. What is her average speed for the whole ride, in miles per hour?

## Hint

Average speed isn't the average of the two speeds. It's the total distance divided by the total time.

## Hint 2

The first part takes 2 hours, and the second takes 3 hours. That's 120 miles in 5 hours.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The first part takes $60 \div 30 = 2$ hours.

**Step 2.** The second part takes $60 \div 20 = 3$ hours.

**Step 3.** That's 120 miles in 5 hours, so her average speed is $120 \div 5 = 24$ miles per hour.

**Answer: (C) 24**
