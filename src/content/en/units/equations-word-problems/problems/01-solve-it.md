If $4x - 7 = 21$, what is the value of $x$?

## Hint

First, undo the "subtract 7." What should you do to both sides?

## Solution

**Step 1.** Add 7 to both sides: $4x = 28$.

**Step 2.** Divide both sides by 4: $x = 7$.

**Check:** $4 \times 7 - 7 = 21$.

**Answer: (C) 7**
