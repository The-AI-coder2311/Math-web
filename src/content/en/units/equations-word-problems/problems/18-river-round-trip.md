A boat travels 30 miles upstream and then 30 miles back downstream. Its speed in still water is 8 miles per hour, and the river flows at 2 miles per hour. How many hours does the round trip take?

## Hint

How does the river change the boat's speed in each direction?

## Hint 2

Upstream, the speed is 6 miles per hour. Downstream, it's 10 miles per hour.

## Solution

Upstream takes $30 \div 6 = 5$ hours. Downstream takes $30 \div 10 = 3$ hours. The total is 8 hours.

**Answer: (C) 8**
