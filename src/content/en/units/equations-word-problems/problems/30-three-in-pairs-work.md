Working in pairs, Al and Bea finish a job in 3 hours, Bea and Cy in 4 hours, and Al and Cy in 6 hours. How many hours would all three take, working together?

## Hint

Add the three pair rates. How many times does each person show up?

## Hint 2

The rates add up to $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{4}$. That's twice the rate of all three.

## Solution

The pair rates add up to $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{4}$. Each person shows up twice, so all three have half of that rate: $\frac{3}{8}$ of the job per hour. So they take $\frac{8}{3}$ hours.

**Answer: (B) $\frac{8}{3}$**
