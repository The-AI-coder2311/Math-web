Ann can paint a fence alone in 6 hours. Together, Ann and Bo can paint it in 4 hours. How many hours would Bo take alone?

## Hint

Write each time as a rate, in fences per hour.

## Hint 2

Bo's rate is the rate together minus Ann's rate.

## Solution

Bo's rate is $\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{12}$ of the fence per hour. So Bo takes 12 hours alone.

**Answer: (D) 12**
