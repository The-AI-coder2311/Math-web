A train passes a pole in 6 seconds, and it crosses a 240-meter bridge in 18 seconds. How fast is the train, in meters per second?

## Hint

What does the extra time cover?

## Hint 2

The extra 12 seconds cover the 240-meter bridge.

## Solution

Passing the pole covers the train's length. Crossing the bridge covers that length plus 240 meters. So the extra 12 seconds cover 240 meters, and the speed is $240 \div 12 = 20$ meters per second.

**Answer: (C) 20**
