A cyclist rides at 40 kilometers per hour for 45 minutes. How many kilometers does the cyclist ride?

## Hint

Write 45 minutes as a fraction of an hour.

## Hint 2

45 minutes is $\frac{3}{4}$ of an hour.

## Solution

Since 45 minutes is $\frac{3}{4}$ of an hour, the distance is $40 \times \frac{3}{4} = 30$ kilometers.

**Answer: (B) 30**
