A car travels at a steady 45 miles per hour for 2 hours and 20 minutes. How many miles does it travel?

## Hint

Twenty minutes is what fraction of an hour?

## Solution

**Step 1.** In the first 2 hours, the car goes $45 \times 2 = 90$ miles.

**Step 2.** Twenty minutes is $\frac{1}{3}$ of an hour, so the car goes another $45 \div 3 = 15$ miles.

**Step 3.** The total is $90 + 15 = 105$ miles.

**Answer: (C) 105**
