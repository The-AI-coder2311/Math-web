What is the slope of the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 11)$?

## Hint

Slope is the change in $y$ over the change in $x$.

## Hint 2

The rise is 9, and the run is 3.

## Solution

The slope is $\frac{11 - 2}{4 - 1} = \frac{9}{3} = 3$.

**Answer: (C) $3$**
