The line $y = 2x + 3$ passes through a point where $x = 5$. What is $y$ at that point?

## Hint

Replace $x$ with 5 in the equation.

## Hint 2

Do the multiplication first: $2 \times 5$.

## Solution

Replacing $x$ with 5 gives $y = 2 \times 5 + 3 = 13$.

**Answer: (D) 13**
