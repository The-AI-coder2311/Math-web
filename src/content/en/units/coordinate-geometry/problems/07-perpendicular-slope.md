A line has a slope of $\frac{2}{3}$. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it?

## Hint

Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle. If one goes uphill, what must the other do?

## Hint 2

Flip the fraction, and change the sign.

## Solution

The slope of a perpendicular line is the negative reciprocal. Flip $\frac{2}{3}$ to get $\frac{3}{2}$, and change the sign to get $-\frac{3}{2}$.

**Check:** The two slopes multiply to $-1$.

**Answer: (A) $-\frac{3}{2}$**
