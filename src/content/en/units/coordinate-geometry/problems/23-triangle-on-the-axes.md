What is the area of the triangle with corners at $(0, 0)$, $(8, 0)$, and $(0, 5)$?

## Hint

The legs lie along the axes.

## Hint 2

The legs are 8 and 5.

## Solution

The triangle has legs of 8 and 5, so its area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 5 = 20$.

**Answer: (B) 20**
