What is the slope of the line through the points $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 11)$?

## Hint

Slope is rise over run. How far does the line go up, and how far does it go across?

## Hint 2

The rise is $11 - 2$, and the run is $4 - 1$.

## Solution

The rise is $11 - 2 = 9$, and the run is $4 - 1 = 3$. So the slope is $9 \div 3 = 3$.

**Answer: (E) 3**
