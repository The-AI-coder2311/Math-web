The line $\frac{x}{6} + \frac{y}{4} = 1$ makes a triangle with the two axes. What is its area?

## Hint

Where does the line cross each axis?

## Hint 2

It crosses at $(6, 0)$ and $(0, 4)$.

## Solution

The line crosses the axes at $(6, 0)$ and $(0, 4)$. So the triangle's area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 4 = 12$.

**Answer: (B) 12**
