How far apart are the points $(-2, 3)$ and $(3, 15)$?

## Hint

Find the horizontal and vertical changes.

## Hint 2

The changes are 5 and 12.

## Solution

The horizontal change is 5, and the vertical change is 12. So the distance is $\sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = 13$.

**Answer: (C) 13**
