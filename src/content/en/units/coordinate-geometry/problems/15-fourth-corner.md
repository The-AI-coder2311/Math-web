Parallelogram $ABCD$ has corners $A(0, 0)$, $B(4, 1)$, and $C(6, 5)$, with the letters in order around the shape. Where is $D$?

## Hint

Side $AD$ is parallel and equal to side $BC$. What slide takes $B$ to $C$?

## Hint 2

The slide from $B$ to $C$ is 2 across and 4 up. Apply it to $A$.

## Solution

The slide from $B$ to $C$ is 2 across and 4 up. Applying the same slide to $A$ gives $D = (2, 4)$.

**Answer: (B) (2, 4)**
