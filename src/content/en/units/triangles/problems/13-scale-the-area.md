Two similar triangles have a scale factor of 3. The small one has an area of 5. What is the area of the large one?

## Hint

If lengths are multiplied by 3, what happens to the base and the height?

## Hint 2

Both get multiplied by 3, so the area is multiplied by $3 \times 3$.

## Solution

The area is multiplied by the scale factor squared: $5 \times 9 = 45$.

**Answer: (E) 45**
