A person 1.5 meters tall casts a 2-meter shadow. At the same moment, a pole casts a 12-meter shadow. How tall is the pole, in meters?

## Hint

How many times longer is the pole's shadow?

## Hint 2

It's 6 times as long, so the pole is 6 times as tall.

## Solution

The pole's shadow is $12 \div 2 = 6$ times as long, so the pole is $1.5 \times 6 = 9$ meters tall.

**Answer: (A) 9**
