In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 5$ and $AC = 10$. The bisector of angle $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$, and $BD = 3$. How long is $DC$?

## Hint

The theorem says that $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}$.

## Hint 2

$AC$ is twice $AB$. So how does $DC$ compare with $BD$?

## Solution

The ratio $AB$ to $AC$ is 1 to 2, so $BD$ to $DC$ is 1 to 2 as well. Since $BD = 3$, we get $DC = 6$.

**Answer: (C) 6**
