A hexagon has 6 sides. What is the sum of its 6 inside angles, in degrees?

## Hint

Pick one corner and draw lines to all the other corners. How many triangles do you make?

## Solution

**Step 1.** Lines from one corner cut a hexagon into $6 - 2 = 4$ triangles.

**Step 2.** Each triangle's angles add up to 180°, so the total is $4 \times 180 = 720$ degrees.

**Answer: (C) 720**
