Triples can be made from $m^2 - n^2$, $2mn$, and $m^2 + n^2$. With $m = 5$ and $n = 1$, what is the hypotenuse?

## Hint

The hypotenuse is the longest side.

## Hint 2

It's $m^2 + n^2$.

## Solution

The hypotenuse is $m^2 + n^2 = 25 + 1 = 26$. The triple is 10-24-26, which is a 5-12-13 triangle doubled.

**Answer: (C) 26**
