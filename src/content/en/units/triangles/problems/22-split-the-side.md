In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 9$, $AC = 15$, and $BC = 16$. The bisector of angle $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$. How long is $BD$?

## Hint

Simplify the ratio $AB$ to $AC$.

## Hint 2

The ratio is 3 to 5. That makes 8 boxes on a side of 16.

## Solution

The ratio is 9 to 15, which is 3 to 5. So $BC$ splits into 8 boxes, and each box holds 2. Since $BD$ goes with $AB$, it's 3 boxes, and $BD = 6$.

**Answer: (A) 6**
