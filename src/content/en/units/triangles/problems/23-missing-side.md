In triangle $ABC$, the bisector of angle $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$, with $BD = 6$ and $DC = 8$. If $AC = 20$, how long is $AB$?

## Hint

$BD$ goes with $AB$, and $DC$ goes with $AC$. Set up the ratio.

## Hint 2

$\frac{6}{8} = \frac{AB}{20}$. Simplify $\frac{6}{8}$ first.

## Solution

The theorem gives $\frac{AB}{20} = \frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4}$. So $AB = 20 \times \frac{3}{4} = 15$.

**Answer: (C) 15**
