A triangle has a height of 20. A line parallel to the base cuts off a small triangle at the top, with $\frac{1}{16}$ of the original area. How far is the line from the base?

## Hint

What's the length ratio, if the area ratio is $\frac{1}{16}$?

## Hint 2

The length ratio is $\frac{1}{4}$, so the small triangle's height is 5.

## Solution

Area scales by the square of the length ratio, so the length ratio is $\frac{1}{4}$. The small triangle's height is 5, so the line is $20 - 5 = 15$ away from the base.

**Answer: (B) 15**
