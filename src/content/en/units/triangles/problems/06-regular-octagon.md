A regular octagon has 8 equal sides and 8 equal angles. How many degrees does each inside angle measure?

## Hint

First, find the sum of all 8 angles. Then share it equally.

## Hint 2

The angles add up to $6 \times 180 = 1080$ degrees. Share that among 8.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The angles add up to $(8 - 2) \times 180 = 1080$ degrees.

**Step 2.** Each of the 8 equal angles is $1080 \div 8 = 135$ degrees.

**Answer: (D) 135**
