Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $144^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

## Hint

What's each exterior angle?

## Hint 2

Each exterior angle is $36^\circ$, and they add up to $360^\circ$.

## Solution

Each exterior angle is $180 - 144 = 36$ degrees. So there are $360 \div 36 = 10$ sides.

**Answer: (C) 10**
