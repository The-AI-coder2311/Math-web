Two angles inside a triangle measure 40° and 65°. One side next to the third angle is extended, which makes an outside angle at that corner. How many degrees does this outside angle measure?

## Hint

The third angle and the outside angle make a straight line. Or you could use the shortcut from the lesson.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The third angle is $180 - 40 - 65 = 75$ degrees.

**Step 2.** The outside angle makes a straight line with it: $180 - 75 = 105$ degrees.

**Shortcut:** The outside angle equals the sum of the two far angles: $40 + 65 = 105$.

**Answer: (C) 105**
