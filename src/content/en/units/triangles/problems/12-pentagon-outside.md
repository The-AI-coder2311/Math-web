What is the measure, in degrees, of each outside angle of a regular pentagon?

## Hint

Walk around the pentagon, turning at each corner. How much do you turn in all?

## Hint 2

You turn a full 360°, shared equally among 5 corners.

## Solution

All of the outside angles add up to 360°, so each one is $360 \div 5 = 72$ degrees.

**Answer: (D) 72**
