A staircase shape has a flat bottom 9 units wide and a straight side 7 units tall. Its steps go up from one end to the other, in sizes you aren't told. What is its perimeter?

## Hint

Imagine sliding the small step edges out to the sides of a rectangle.

## Hint 2

The edges slide into the outline of a 9 by 7 rectangle.

## Solution

The horizontal step edges add up to the width, and the vertical edges add up to the height. So the perimeter is the same as that of a 9 by 7 rectangle: $2 \times (9 + 7) = 32$.

**Answer: (B) 32**
