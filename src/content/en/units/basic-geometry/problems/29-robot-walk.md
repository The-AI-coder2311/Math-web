A robot moves 1 unit north, 6 units east, 7 units north, and 9 units east. How far is it from where it started, in a straight line?

## Hint

Add up the total distance north and the total distance east.

## Hint 2

North is 8, and east is 15. These are the legs of a right triangle.

## Solution

The total north is 8, and the total east is 15. So the distance is $\sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{289} = 17$.

**Answer: (A) 17**
