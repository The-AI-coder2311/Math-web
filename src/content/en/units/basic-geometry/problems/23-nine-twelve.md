A right triangle has legs of 9 and 12. How long is its hypotenuse?

## Hint

Use $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

## Hint 2

$81 + 144 = 225$.

## Solution

We have $9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225$, so the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{225} = 15$. It's a 3-4-5 triangle, scaled by 3.

**Answer: (A) 15**
