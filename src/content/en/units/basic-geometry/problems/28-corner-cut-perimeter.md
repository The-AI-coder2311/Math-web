A 4 by 3 rectangle is cut out of one corner of a 10 by 8 rectangle. What is the perimeter of the shape that's left?

## Hint

Compare the new edges with the pieces of the outline that were removed.

## Hint 2

The new edges have the same lengths as the removed pieces.

## Solution

The cut removes a piece of 4 and a piece of 3 from the outline, and it adds new edges of 4 and 3. So the perimeter doesn't change: $2 \times (10 + 8) = 36$.

**Answer: (B) 36**
