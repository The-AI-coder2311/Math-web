A square has a perimeter of 36. What is its area?

## Hint

Find the length of one side first.

## Hint 2

Four equal sides make 36, so each side is 9.

## Solution

Each side is $36 \div 4 = 9$, so the area is $9 \times 9 = 81$.

**Answer: (D) 81**
