A right triangle has legs of 6 and 8, and a hypotenuse of 10. How long is the height from the right angle to the hypotenuse?

## Hint

Find the area in two different ways.

## Hint 2

Using the legs, the area is 24. Using the hypotenuse, it's $\frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times h$.

## Solution

Using the legs, the area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = 24$. Using the hypotenuse as the base, $\frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times h = 24$, so $h = 4.8$.

**Answer: (B) 4.8**
