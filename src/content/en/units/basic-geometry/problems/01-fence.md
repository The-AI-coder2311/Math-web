A rectangular dog pen is 9 feet long and 4 feet wide. How many feet of fence does it take to go all the way around the pen?

## Hint

Draw the rectangle and label all four sides. Going around means you add all four.

## Solution

Going all the way around means adding all four sides:

$$9 + 4 + 9 + 4 = 26$$

**Answer: (C) 26**
