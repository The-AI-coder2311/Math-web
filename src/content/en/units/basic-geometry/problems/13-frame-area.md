A 5 by 7 photo has a frame around it that is 1 inch wide on every side. What is the area of the frame, in square inches?

## Hint

Draw it. What are the outer measurements of the frame?

## Hint 2

The frame adds 1 inch on both sides, so the outside is 7 by 9. Subtract the photo.

## Solution

The outside is $7 \times 9 = 63$. The photo is $5 \times 7 = 35$. So the frame is $63 - 35 = 28$.

**Answer: (C) 28**
