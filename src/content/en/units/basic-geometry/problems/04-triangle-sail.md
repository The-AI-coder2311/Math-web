A triangular sail has a base of 10 feet and a height of 7 feet. What is its area, in square feet?

## Hint

A triangle fills half of the rectangle drawn around it. What's the area of that rectangle?

## Solution

**Step 1.** The rectangle around the triangle has an area of $10 \times 7 = 70$ square feet.

**Step 2.** The triangle is half of that: $70 \div 2 = 35$ square feet.

**Answer: (C) 35**
