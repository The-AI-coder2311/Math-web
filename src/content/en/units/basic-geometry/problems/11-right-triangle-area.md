A right triangle has legs of length 8 and 5. What is its area?

## Hint

In a right triangle, which sides can act as the base and the height?

## Hint 2

The legs meet at a right angle, so one is the base and the other is the height.

## Solution

The legs are the base and the height, so the area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 5 = 20$.

**Answer: (A) 20**
