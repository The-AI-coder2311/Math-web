Two angles of a triangle measure $40^\circ$ and $75^\circ$. How many degrees are in the third angle?

## Hint

The angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$.

## Hint 2

$40 + 75 = 115$.

## Solution

The two angles add up to 115, so the third is $180 - 115 = 65$ degrees.

**Answer: (B) 65**
