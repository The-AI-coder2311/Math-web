In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ is on side $BC$, with $BD$ to $DC$ in a ratio of 3 to 2. The area of triangle $ABC$ is 50. What is the area of triangle $ABD$?

## Hint

Triangles $ABD$ and $ADC$ share a height from $A$.

## Hint 2

So their areas are in a ratio of 3 to 2.

## Solution

The two small triangles share a height, so their areas are in the same ratio as their bases, 3 to 2. There are 5 boxes in a total of 50, so each box holds 10. Triangle $ABD$ fills 3 boxes, so its area is 30.

**Answer: (C) 30**
