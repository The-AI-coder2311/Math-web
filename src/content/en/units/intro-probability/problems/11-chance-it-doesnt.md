The probability of drawing a red card from a stack is $\frac{3}{8}$. What is the probability of **not** drawing a red card?

## Hint

Something either happens or it doesn't. What do those two probabilities add up to?

## Hint 2

They add up to 1. Subtract $\frac{3}{8}$ from 1.

## Solution

The two probabilities add up to 1, so the answer is $1 - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{5}{8}$**
