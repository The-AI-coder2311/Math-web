The mean of 4 numbers is 9. Three of them are 7, 8, and 10. What is the fourth number?

## Hint

What do the 4 numbers add up to?

## Hint 2

The total is $4 \times 9 = 36$.

## Solution

The total is $4 \times 9 = 36$. The three given numbers add up to 25. So the fourth is $36 - 25 = 11$.

**Answer: (D) 11**
