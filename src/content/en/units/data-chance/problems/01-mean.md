A class records the number of books read by 5 students: 12, 15, 9, 20, and 14. What is the mean number of books read?

## Hint

Add up all the numbers. What do you divide by?

## Solution

**Step 1.** Add them up: $12 + 15 + 9 + 20 + 14 = 70$.

**Step 2.** Divide by the number of students: $70 \div 5 = 14$.

**Answer: (B) 14**
