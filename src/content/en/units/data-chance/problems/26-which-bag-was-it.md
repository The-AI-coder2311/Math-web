Bag A has 4 red marbles and 1 blue marble. Bag B has 1 red marble and 4 blue marbles. You pick a bag at random and draw a red marble. What is the probability that you picked Bag A?

## Hint

Imagine all 10 marbles in a pile. How many are red?

## Hint 2

Five are red. How many of those came from Bag A?

## Solution

The bags are the same size, so each marble is equally likely. Of the 5 red marbles, 4 came from Bag A. So the probability is $\frac{4}{5}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{4}{5}$**
