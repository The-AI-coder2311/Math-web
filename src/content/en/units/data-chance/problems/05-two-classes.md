A class of 12 students has a mean score of 90 on a quiz. Another class of 36 students has a mean score of 70 on the same quiz. What is the mean score of all 48 students together?

## Hint

The classes aren't the same size, so you can't take the number halfway between. Find the total points.

## Hint 2

The totals are 1,080 and 2,520 points, for 48 students.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The first class scored $12 \times 90 = 1080$ points in all.

**Step 2.** The second class scored $36 \times 70 = 2520$ points in all.

**Step 3.** The overall mean is $(1080 + 2520) \div 48 = 75$.

**Answer: (B) 75**
