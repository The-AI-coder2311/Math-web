The mean of Tara's 6 test scores is 80. Her teacher drops her lowest score, which is 70. What is the mean of the remaining 5 scores?

## Hint

Think in totals. What do the 6 scores add up to?

## Solution

**Step 1.** The total of all 6 scores is $6 \times 80 = 480$.

**Step 2.** Removing the 70 leaves $480 - 70 = 410$.

**Step 3.** The new mean is $410 \div 5 = 82$.

**Answer: (D) 82**
