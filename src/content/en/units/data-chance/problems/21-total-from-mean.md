The mean of 5 numbers is 12. What is their sum?

## Hint

The mean is the sum divided by how many numbers there are.

## Hint 2

Multiply the mean by how many numbers there are.

## Solution

The sum is $5 \times 12 = 60$.

**Answer: (C) 60**
