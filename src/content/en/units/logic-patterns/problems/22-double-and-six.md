I think of a number, double it, and add 6. The result is 20. What is my number?

## Hint

Undo each step, starting from the last.

## Hint 2

First subtract 6, then halve.

## Solution

Undo the steps in reverse order: $20 - 6 = 14$, and $14 \div 2 = 7$.

**Answer: (A) 7**
