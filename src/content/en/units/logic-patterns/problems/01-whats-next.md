What number comes next in this list?

$$2, \ 5, \ 10, \ 17, \ 26, \ \ldots$$

## Hint

Look at how much the list grows at each step. What do you notice about those amounts?

## Solution

**Step 1.** Find the jumps: 5 is 3 more than 2, 10 is 5 more than 5, 17 is 7 more than 10, and 26 is 9 more than 17.

**Step 2.** The jumps are the odd numbers 3, 5, 7, and 9, so the next jump is 11.

**Step 3.** The next number is $26 + 11 = 37$.

**Answer: (C) 37**
