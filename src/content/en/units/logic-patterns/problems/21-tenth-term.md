The pattern 3, 7, 11, 15, ... adds 4 each time. What is the 10th number?

## Hint

How many jumps of 4 get you from the 1st number to the 10th?

## Hint 2

There are 9 jumps.

## Solution

From the 1st number to the 10th, there are 9 jumps of 4. So the 10th number is $3 + 9 \times 4 = 39$.

**Answer: (B) 39**
