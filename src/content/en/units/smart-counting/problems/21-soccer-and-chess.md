In a class, 18 students play soccer, 15 play chess, and 8 do both. How many students do at least one of the two?

## Hint

Adding 18 and 15 counts some students twice. Which ones?

## Hint 2

Subtract the 8 who were counted twice.

## Solution

Adding the groups counts the 8 students in both twice. So the count is $18 + 15 - 8 = 25$.

**Answer: (B) 25**
