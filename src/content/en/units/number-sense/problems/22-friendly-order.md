What is $25 \times 16 \times 4$?

## Hint

Which two numbers make a friendly product?

## Hint 2

$25 \times 4 = 100$.

## Solution

Multiply the friendly pair first: $25 \times 4 = 100$. Then $100 \times 16 = 1600$.

**Answer: (B) 1600**
