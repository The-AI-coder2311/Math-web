What is the value of $101 \times 99 - 100 \times 98$?

## Hint

Split up each product using the distributive property. Try writing $101 \times 99$ as $101 \times 100 - 101$.

## Hint 2

$101 \times 99 = 9999$, and $100 \times 98 = 9800$.

## Solution

**Step 1.** $101 \times 99 = 101 \times 100 - 101 = 9999$.

**Step 2.** $100 \times 98 = 9800$.

**Step 3.** $9999 - 9800 = 199$.

**Answer: (B) 199**
