What is the value of $46 \times 99$?

## Hint

The number 99 is close to a round number. Use that number, and then fix it.

## Hint 2

$46 \times 100 = 4600$. Is that too many or too few? By how much?

## Solution

$46 \times 100 = 4600$, which is one 46 too many. So $4600 - 46 = 4554$.

**Answer: (A) 4554**
