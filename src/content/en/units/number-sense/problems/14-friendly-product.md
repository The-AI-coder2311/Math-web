What is the value of $4 \times 23 \times 25$?

## Hint

You can multiply in any order. Which two numbers make a friendly product?

## Hint 2

$4 \times 25 = 100$.

## Solution

Multiply the friendly pair first: $4 \times 25 = 100$. Then $100 \times 23 = 2300$.

**Answer: (D) 2300**
