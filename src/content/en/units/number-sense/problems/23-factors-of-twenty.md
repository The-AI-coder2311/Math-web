How many factors does 20 have?

## Hint

List the pairs of numbers that multiply to 20.

## Hint 2

The pairs start with 1 and 20.

## Solution

The factor pairs are 1 and 20, 2 and 10, and 4 and 5. That makes 6 factors.

**Answer: (C) 6**
