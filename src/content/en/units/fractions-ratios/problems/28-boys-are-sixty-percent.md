In a class, 40% of the students are girls, and the other 12 students are boys. How many girls are in the class?

## Hint

What percent of the class is boys?

## Hint 2

Boys are 60% of the class. If 60% is 12 students, what's 20%?

## Solution

Boys are 60% of the class, so 60% is 12 students. That makes 20% equal to 4 students, and 40% equal to 8 students.

**Answer: (A) 8**
