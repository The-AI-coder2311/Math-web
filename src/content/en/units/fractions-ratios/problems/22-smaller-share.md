Two friends share 35 stickers in a ratio of 2 to 5. How many stickers does the friend with the smaller share get?

## Hint

How many boxes are there in all?

## Hint 2

There are 7 boxes in a total of 35. What's in one box?

## Solution

There are $2 + 5 = 7$ boxes, so each box holds $35 \div 7 = 5$ stickers. The smaller share fills 2 boxes, which is 10 stickers.

**Answer: (A) 10**
