What is 15% of 60?

## Hint

Find 10% first, then 5%.

## Hint 2

10% of 60 is 6, and 5% is half of that.

## Solution

10% of 60 is 6, and 5% of 60 is 3. So 15% is $6 + 3 = 9$.

**Answer: (B) 9**
