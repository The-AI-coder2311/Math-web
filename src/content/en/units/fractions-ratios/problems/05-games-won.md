A soccer team played 45 games and won 20% of them. How many games did the team win?

## Hint

Find 10% first, which means dividing by 10. How can you get from 10% to 20%?

## Solution

**Step 1.** 10% of 45 is $45 \div 10 = 4.5$.

**Step 2.** 20% is twice as much: $2 \times 4.5 = 9$.

**Answer: (B) 9**
