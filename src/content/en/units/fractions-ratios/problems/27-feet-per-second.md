A car travels at 60 miles per hour. How many feet does it travel each second? (One mile is 5,280 feet.)

## Hint

At 60 miles per hour, how far does it go in one minute?

## Hint 2

It goes 1 mile, or 5,280 feet, per minute. A minute has 60 seconds.

## Solution

At 60 miles per hour, the car goes 1 mile per minute, which is 5,280 feet per minute. Dividing by 60 gives $5280 \div 60 = 88$ feet per second.

**Answer: (C) 88**
