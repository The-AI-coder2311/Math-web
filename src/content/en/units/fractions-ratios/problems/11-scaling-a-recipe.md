A recipe uses 2 cups of flour to make 12 cookies. How many cups of flour are needed to make 30 cookies?

## Hint

How many cookies does 1 cup make?

## Hint 2

One cup makes 6 cookies. How many groups of 6 make 30?

## Solution

One cup makes $12 \div 2 = 6$ cookies. So 30 cookies need $30 \div 6 = 5$ cups.

**Answer: (E) 5**
