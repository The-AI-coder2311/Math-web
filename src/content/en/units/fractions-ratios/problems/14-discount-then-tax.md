A \$50 shirt is on sale for 30% off. Then a 10% tax is added to the sale price. What is the final price, in dollars?

## Hint

Do one step at a time. What's the sale price?

## Hint 2

The sale price is \$35. The tax is 10% of that.

## Solution

**Step 1.** 30% of 50 is 15, so the sale price is $50 - 15 = 35$.

**Step 2.** 10% of 35 is 3.5, so the final price is $35 + 3.5 = 38.5$.

**Answer: (A) 38.5**
