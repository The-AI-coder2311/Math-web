Ana eats $\frac{1}{2}$ of a pizza, and Ben eats $\frac{1}{3}$ of the same pizza. What fraction of the pizza is left?

## Hint

Halves and thirds are different-sized pieces. What size of slice could you cut so that both fit? Try sixths.

## Hint 2

Cut the pizza into 6 slices. Ana eats 3 and Ben eats 2.

## Solution

**Step 1.** Imagine the pizza cut into 6 equal slices, since 6 is a multiple of both 2 and 3.

**Step 2.** Ana eats half of the 6 slices, which is 3 slices. Ben eats a third of the 6 slices, which is 2 slices.

**Step 3.** Together they eat $3 + 2 = 5$ slices, so 1 of the 6 slices is left.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{1}{6}$**
