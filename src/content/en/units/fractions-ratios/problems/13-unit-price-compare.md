Store A sells 6 pens for \$3. Store B sells 10 pens for \$4.50. How many cents cheaper per pen is Store B?

## Hint

Find the price of one pen at each store.

## Hint 2

Store A charges 50 cents per pen. Divide to find the price at Store B.

## Solution

Store A charges $300 \div 6 = 50$ cents per pen. Store B charges $450 \div 10 = 45$ cents. The difference is $50 - 45 = 5$ cents.

**Answer: (B) 5**
