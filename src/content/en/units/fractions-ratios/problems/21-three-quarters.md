What is $\frac{3}{4}$ of 28?

## Hint

First find one fourth of 28.

## Hint 2

One fourth of 28 is 7. How many fourths do you need?

## Solution

One fourth of 28 is $28 \div 4 = 7$, and three fourths is $3 \times 7 = 21$.

**Answer: (C) 21**
