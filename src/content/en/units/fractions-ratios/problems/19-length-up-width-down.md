A rectangle's length is increased by 25%, and its width is decreased by 20%. By what percent does its area change?

## Hint

The answer doesn't depend on the sides, so pick friendly numbers. Try a rectangle that's 10 by 10.

## Hint 2

The new sides are 12.5 and 8. What's the new area?

## Solution

Start with a 10 by 10 rectangle, with an area of 100. The new sides are 12.5 and 8, and $12.5 \times 8 = 100$. The area is the same, so it changes by 0%.

**Answer: (A) 0**
