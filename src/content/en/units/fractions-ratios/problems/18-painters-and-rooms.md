Four painters can paint 3 identical rooms in 6 hours. Working at the same rate, how many hours would 6 painters take to paint 9 of these rooms?

## Hint

Count painter-hours. How many painter-hours does one room take?

## Hint 2

Four painters for 6 hours is 24 painter-hours for 3 rooms. So one room takes 8 painter-hours.

## Solution

**Step 1.** $4 \times 6 = 24$ painter-hours paint 3 rooms, so one room takes $24 \div 3 = 8$ painter-hours.

**Step 2.** Nine rooms take $9 \times 8 = 72$ painter-hours.

**Step 3.** With 6 painters, that's $72 \div 6 = 12$ hours.

**Answer: (E) 12**
