What is $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{6}$?

## Hint

Fourths and sixths are different-sized pieces. What size fits both?

## Hint 2

Use twelfths. $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12}$ and $\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{12}$.

## Solution

Using twelfths, $\frac{9}{12} + \frac{2}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{11}{12}$**
