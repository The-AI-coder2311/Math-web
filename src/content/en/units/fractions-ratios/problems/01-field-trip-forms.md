A class has 27 students, and $\frac{2}{3}$ of them have turned in their field trip forms. How many students have turned in their forms?

## Hint

Split 27 into 3 equal groups first. Then decide how many of those groups to take.

## Solution

**Step 1.** Split 27 students into 3 equal groups: $27 \div 3 = 9$. Each group has 9 students.

**Step 2.** Take 2 of those groups: $2 \times 9 = 18$.

**Answer: (C) 18**
