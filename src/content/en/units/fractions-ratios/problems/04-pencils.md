Five pencils cost \$1.25 in all. Each pencil costs the same amount. How many dollars do 8 pencils cost?

## Hint

Start by finding the cost of one pencil.

## Solution

**Step 1.** One pencil costs $1.25 \div 5 = 0.25$, which is 25 cents.

**Step 2.** Eight pencils cost $8 \times 0.25 = 2$, which is 2 dollars.

**Answer: (B) 2**
