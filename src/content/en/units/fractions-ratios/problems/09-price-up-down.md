A jacket costs \$80. The store raises the price by 25%. Later, the store puts the jacket on sale for 25% off the new price. What is the sale price, in dollars?

## Hint

Do one change at a time. The 25% off is taken from the new, higher price.

## Solution

**Step 1.** 25% of 80 is $80 \div 4 = 20$, so the new price is $80 + 20 = 100$ dollars.

**Step 2.** 25% of the new price is $100 \div 4 = 25$, so the sale price is $100 - 25 = 75$ dollars.

The price doesn't return to \$80, because the two percents are taken from different numbers.

**Answer: (C) 75**
