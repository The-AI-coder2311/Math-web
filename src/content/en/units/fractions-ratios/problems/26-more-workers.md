Six workers can finish a job in 12 days. At the same rate, how many days will 9 workers take?

## Hint

How many worker-days does the job take in all?

## Hint 2

The job takes $6 \times 12 = 72$ worker-days.

## Solution

The job takes $6 \times 12 = 72$ worker-days. With 9 workers, it takes $72 \div 9 = 8$ days.

**Answer: (B) 8**
