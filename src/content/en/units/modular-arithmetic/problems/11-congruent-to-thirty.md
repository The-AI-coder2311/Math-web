What remainder does 30 leave when divided by 7? In symbols, which number from 0 to 6 goes in the box: $30 \equiv \square \pmod{7}$?

## Hint

What remainder does 30 leave when divided by 7?

## Hint 2

$7 \times 4 = 28$.

## Solution

Since $30 = 7 \times 4 + 2$, the remainder is 2.

**Answer: (B) 2**
