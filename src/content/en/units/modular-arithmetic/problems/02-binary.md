What is the number $1011_2$, written in base 10?

## Hint

In base 2, the places from right to left are worth 1, 2, 4, and 8.

## Solution

Multiply each digit by its place value:

$$1 \times 8 + 0 \times 4 + 1 \times 2 + 1 \times 1 = 11$$

**Answer: (C) 11**
