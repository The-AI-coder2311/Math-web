Write $0.555...$ as a fraction.

## Hint

A repeating block of 1 digit sits over 9.

## Hint 2

The series has a first term of $\frac{5}{10}$, and a ratio of $\frac{1}{10}$.

## Solution

The series has a first term of $\frac{5}{10}$ and a ratio of $\frac{1}{10}$. Its sum is $\frac{5}{10} \div \frac{9}{10} = \frac{5}{9}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{5}{9}$**
