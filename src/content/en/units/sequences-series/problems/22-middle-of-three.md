Three numbers in an arithmetic sequence add up to 30. What is the middle number?

## Hint

Call the numbers $a - d$, $a$, and $a + d$.

## Hint 2

The $d$'s cancel when you add.

## Solution

The sum is $(a - d) + a + (a + d) = 3a = 30$, so $a = 10$.

**Answer: (B) 10**
