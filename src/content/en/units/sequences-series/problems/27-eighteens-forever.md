Write $0.181818...$ as a fraction in lowest terms.

## Hint

A repeating block of 2 digits sits over 99.

## Hint 2

Simplify $\frac{18}{99}$.

## Solution

The repeating block is 18, so the number is $\frac{18}{99} = \frac{2}{11}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{2}{11}$**
