An angle has its corner on a circle, and its two sides reach the circle at points $A$ and $B$. It measures 50°, and the center of the circle is inside the angle. How many degrees is angle $AOB$, where $O$ is the center?

## Hint

Which is larger: the angle at the center, or the one at the edge?

## Hint 2

The center angle is twice as large.

## Solution

The center angle is twice the angle at the edge: $2 \times 50 = 100$ degrees.

**Answer: (B) 100**
