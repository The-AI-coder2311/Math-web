What is the slope of the line through $(2, 3)$ and $(6, 11)$?

## Hint

Slope is rise over run.

## Hint 2

The rise is 8, and the run is 4.

## Solution

The rise is $11 - 3 = 8$, and the run is $6 - 2 = 4$. The slope is $8 \div 4 = 2$.

**Answer: (C) 2**
