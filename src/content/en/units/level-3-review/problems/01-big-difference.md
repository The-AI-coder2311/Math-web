What is the value of $61^2 - 39^2$?

## Hint

Use the difference of squares pattern.

## Hint 2

$(61 + 39)(61 - 39)$.

## Solution

$61^2 - 39^2 = (61 + 39)(61 - 39) = 100 \times 22 = 2200$.

**Answer: (D) 2200**
