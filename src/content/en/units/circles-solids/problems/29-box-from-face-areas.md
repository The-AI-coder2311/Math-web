A box has faces with areas of 12, 15, and 20. What is its volume?

## Hint

Call the sides $a$, $b$, and $c$. Then $ab = 12$, $bc = 15$, and $ca = 20$.

## Hint 2

What do you get when you multiply those three equations?

## Solution

Multiplying gives $(abc)^2 = 12 \times 15 \times 20 = 3600$. So the volume is $abc = 60$. The sides are 3, 4, and 5.

**Answer: (B) 60**
