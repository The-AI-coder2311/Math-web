What is the area of a circle with a radius of 6?

## Hint

Use the area formula, $\pi r^2$. Be sure to square only the radius.

## Hint 2

Square the radius: $6 \times 6 = 36$.

## Solution

The area is $\pi \times 6^2$. Since $6^2 = 36$, the area is $36\pi$.

**Answer: (D) $36\pi$**
