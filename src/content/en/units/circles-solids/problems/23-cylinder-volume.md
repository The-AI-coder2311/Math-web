A cylinder has a radius of 2 and a height of 5. What is its volume?

## Hint

The volume is the area of the base times the height.

## Hint 2

The base is a circle with an area of $4\pi$.

## Solution

The base has an area of $\pi \times 2^2 = 4\pi$. Multiplying by the height gives $4\pi \times 5 = 20\pi$.

**Answer: (C) $20\pi$**
