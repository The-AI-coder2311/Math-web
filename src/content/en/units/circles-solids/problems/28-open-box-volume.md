A square with sides of 3 is cut from each corner of a 14 by 14 sheet of cardboard, and the flaps are folded up to make an open box. What is its volume?

## Hint

What are the height and the base side of the box?

## Hint 2

The height is 3. The base side is $14 - 2 \times 3$.

## Solution

The height is 3, and the base side is $14 - 6 = 8$. The volume is $8 \times 8 \times 3 = 192$.

**Answer: (C) 192**
