A fish tank has a rectangular base that is 8 inches by 10 inches, and it's partly filled with water. A solid metal cube with sides of 4 inches is lowered into the tank and is completely underwater, with no water spilling out. How many inches does the water level rise?

## Hint

The cube pushes aside water equal to its own volume. That extra volume is shaped like a flat box on top of the water. What's its base?

## Hint 2

The cube pushes aside 64 cubic inches. That water spreads over a base of 80 square inches.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The cube's volume is $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$ cubic inches, so it pushes aside 64 cubic inches of water.

**Step 2.** That water forms a thin layer on top, with a base of $8 \times 10 = 80$ square inches.

**Step 3.** A box's volume is its base times its height, so $80 \times \text{rise} = 64$. The rise is $64 \div 80 = 0.8$ inches.

**Answer: (C) 0.8**
