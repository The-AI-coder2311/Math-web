A pie has a radius of 4 inches. A slice is cut with an angle of 90° at the center. What is the area of the top of the slice, in square inches?

## Hint

What fraction of the whole circle is 90° out of 360°?

## Hint 2

The slice is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the pie, and the pie's area is $16\pi$.

## Solution

**Step 1.** The whole pie has an area of $\pi \times 4^2$, which is $16\pi$.

**Step 2.** The slice is $\frac{90}{360} = \frac{1}{4}$ of the pie.

**Step 3.** One fourth of $16\pi$ is $4\pi$.

**Answer: (E) $4\pi$**
