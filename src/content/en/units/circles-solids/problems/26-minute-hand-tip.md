The minute hand of a clock is 12 centimeters long. How far does its tip travel in 10 minutes, in centimeters?

## Hint

What fraction of a full turn is 10 minutes?

## Hint 2

It's $\frac{1}{6}$ of a turn. A full turn is $24\pi$.

## Solution

A full turn covers $2\pi \times 12 = 24\pi$. Ten minutes is $\frac{1}{6}$ of a turn, so the tip travels $4\pi$ centimeters.

**Answer: (B) $4\pi$**
