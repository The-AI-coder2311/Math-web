A goat is tied to a corner of a square barn with sides of 6 meters, using a 6-meter rope. It can't go into the barn. How much ground can it graze, in square meters?

## Hint

The barn's corner blocks part of the circle. What fraction?

## Hint 2

The goat can reach $\frac{3}{4}$ of a circle of radius 6. Can the rope bend around the next corner?

## Solution

The goat can reach $\frac{3}{4}$ of a circle of radius 6, which is $\frac{3}{4} \times 36\pi = 27\pi$. The rope is exactly as long as the barn's side, so there's none left to swing around the next corner.

**Answer: (B) $27\pi$**
