A box measures 2 by 3 by 6. What is its surface area?

## Hint

A box has 3 pairs of matching faces. What are their sizes?

## Hint 2

The pairs are 2 by 3, 2 by 6, and 3 by 6.

## Solution

The faces have areas of 6, 12, and 18, and each appears twice. So the surface area is $2 \times (6 + 12 + 18) = 72$.

**Answer: (C) 72**
