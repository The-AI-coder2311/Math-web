A belt connects a wheel of radius 6 to a wheel of radius 3. When the big wheel turns 4 times, how many times does the small wheel turn?

## Hint

The belt moves the same distance along both wheels.

## Hint 2

Turns times radius stays the same.

## Solution

Turns times radius stays the same, so $4 \times 6 = 24$. The small wheel turns $24 \div 3 = 8$ times.

**Answer: (C) 8**
