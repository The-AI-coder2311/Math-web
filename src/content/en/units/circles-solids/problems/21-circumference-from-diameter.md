A circle has a diameter of 10. What is its circumference?

## Hint

The circumference is $\pi$ times the diameter.

## Hint 2

Multiply 10 by $\pi$.

## Solution

The circumference is $\pi \times 10 = 10\pi$.

**Answer: (B) $10\pi$**
