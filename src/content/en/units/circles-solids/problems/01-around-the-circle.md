A circular pond has a diameter of 10 meters. What is the distance around the pond, in meters?

## Hint

The distance around is the circumference, which is $2 \pi r$. What's the radius?

## Solution

**Step 1.** The radius is half of the diameter: $10 \div 2 = 5$.

**Step 2.** The circumference is $2 \times 5 \times \pi$, which is $10\pi$ meters.

**Answer: (B) $10\pi$**
