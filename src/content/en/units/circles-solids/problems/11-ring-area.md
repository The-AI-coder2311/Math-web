Two circles share the same center, with radii of 7 and 5. What is the area of the ring between them?

## Hint

What's the whole, and what's missing?

## Hint 2

The big circle's area is $49\pi$, and the small circle's area is $25\pi$.

## Solution

Subtract the areas: $49\pi - 25\pi = 24\pi$.

**Answer: (E) $24\pi$**
