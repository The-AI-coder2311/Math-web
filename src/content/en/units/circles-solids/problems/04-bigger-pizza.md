A large pizza has a diameter of 12 inches, and a small pizza has a diameter of 6 inches. They have the same thickness. The large pizza has how many times as much pizza as the small one?

## Hint

Compare the areas, not the widths. What's the radius of each pizza?

## Solution

**Step 1.** The large pizza has a radius of 6, so its area is $36\pi$.

**Step 2.** The small pizza has a radius of 3, so its area is $9\pi$.

**Step 3.** Compare them: $36 \div 9 = 4$. So the large pizza has 4 times as much, even though it's only twice as wide.

**Answer: (C) 4**
