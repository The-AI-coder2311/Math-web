A slice of a circle with a radius of 3 has an angle of 120°. What is its area?

## Hint

What fraction of the whole circle is 120° out of 360°?

## Hint 2

It's $\frac{1}{3}$ of the circle, and the whole area is $9\pi$.

## Solution

The slice is $\frac{120}{360} = \frac{1}{3}$ of the circle. The whole area is $9\pi$, so the slice has an area of $3\pi$.

**Answer: (E) $3\pi$**
