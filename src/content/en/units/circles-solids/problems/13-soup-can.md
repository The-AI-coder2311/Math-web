A can has a radius of 2 and a height of 5. What is its volume?

## Hint

What's the area of the circle on the bottom? How many layers are there?

## Hint 2

The base has an area of $4\pi$, and there are 5 layers.

## Solution

The base has an area of $\pi \times 2^2$, which is $4\pi$. With a height of 5, the volume is $20\pi$.

**Answer: (D) $20\pi$**
