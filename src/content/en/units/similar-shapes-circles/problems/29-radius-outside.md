A triangle has sides 13, 13, and 10. Its area is 60. What is the radius of the circle around it?

## Hint

Use $R = \frac{abc}{4A}$.

## Hint 2

$R = \frac{13 \times 13 \times 10}{4 \times 60}$.

## Solution

$R = \frac{13 \times 13 \times 10}{4 \times 60} = \frac{1690}{240} = \frac{169}{24}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{169}{24}$**
