A chord cuts off an arc of $80^\circ$. A tangent touches the circle at one end of the chord. How many degrees are in the angle between the tangent and the chord?

## Hint

This angle acts like an inscribed angle.

## Hint 2

It's half of the arc.

## Solution

The angle between a tangent and a chord is half of the arc it cuts off. So it's $80 \div 2 = 40$ degrees.

**Answer: (B) 40**
