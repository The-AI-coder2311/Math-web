The circle inside a triangle touches the sides, making tangent lengths of 3, 5, and 12 from the three corners. What is the radius of the circle?

## Hint

Half the perimeter is the sum of the tangent lengths.

## Hint 2

$s = 20$, and $A = \sqrt{20 \times 3 \times 5 \times 12}$.

## Solution

Here, $s = 3 + 5 + 12 = 20$. The area is $\sqrt{20 \times 3 \times 5 \times 12} = \sqrt{3600} = 60$. So $r = \frac{60}{20} = 3$. The triangle has sides of 8, 15, and 17.

**Answer: (B) 3**
