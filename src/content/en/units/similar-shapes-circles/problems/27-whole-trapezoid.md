A trapezoid has parallel sides of 2 and 4. Its diagonals cross, making four triangles. The triangle on the short side has an area of 3. What is the area of the whole trapezoid?

## Hint

With a ratio of 1 to $k$, the areas are in the ratio $1$, $k$, $k$, and $k^2$.

## Hint 2

Here, $k = 2$.

## Solution

The ratio of the sides is 1 to 2. So the areas are 3, 6, 6, and 12, which add up to 27.

**Answer: (C) 27**
