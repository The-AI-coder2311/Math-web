A trapezoid has parallel sides of 5 and 15. Its diagonals cross. What is the ratio of the area of the triangle on the short side to the area of the triangle on the long side?

## Hint

Those two triangles are similar. What's their ratio of lengths?

## Hint 2

The ratio of lengths is 1 to 3. Areas use the square.

## Solution

The two triangles are similar, with lengths in a ratio of 5 to 15, which is 1 to 3. So their areas are in a ratio of 1 to 9.

**Answer: (B) 1 to 9**
