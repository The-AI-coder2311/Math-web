Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral and sits inside a circle. Point $P$ lies on the arc between $B$ and $C$. If $PB = 4$ and $PC = 7$, how long is $PA$?

## Hint

Use Ptolemy's theorem on $A$, $B$, $P$, and $C$.

## Hint 2

The side $s$ cancels.

## Solution

Ptolemy's theorem gives $PA \times s = PB \times s + PC \times s$. Each $s$ cancels, so $PA = 4 + 7 = 11$.

**Answer: (C) 11**
