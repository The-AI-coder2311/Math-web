A circle with a radius of 4 sits inside a triangle, touching all three sides. The triangle's perimeter is 30. What is its area?

## Hint

Use $A = rs$. What's $s$?

## Hint 2

$s$ is half of the perimeter, so it's 15.

## Solution

Half the perimeter is $s = 15$, so $A = rs = 4 \times 15 = 60$.

**Answer: (D) 60**
