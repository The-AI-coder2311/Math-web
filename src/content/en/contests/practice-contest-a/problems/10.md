Juice and water are mixed in a ratio of 3 to 5 to make 40 cups of punch. How many cups of juice are used?

## Solution

There are 8 boxes, so each holds 5 cups. Juice fills 3 boxes: $3 \times 5 = 15$.

**Answer: (B) 15**
