In triangle $ABC$, a line parallel to $BC$ crosses $AB$ at $D$ and $AC$ at $E$. If $AD = 2$, $DB = 4$, and triangle $ADE$ has an area of 3, what is the area of the four-sided region $DBCE$?

## Solution

The scale factor is $6 \div 2 = 3$, so the whole triangle has an area of $3 \times 9 = 27$. The region is $27 - 3 = 24$.

**Answer: (D) 24**
