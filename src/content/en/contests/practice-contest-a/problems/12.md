Two standard dice are rolled. What is the probability that the numbers add up to 6?

## Solution

The pairs are (1, 5), (2, 4), (3, 3), (4, 2), and (5, 1). That's 5 out of 36.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{5}{36}$**
