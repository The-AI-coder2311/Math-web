What is the median of 20, 3, 12, 25, 8, and 7?

## Solution

In order, the numbers are 3, 7, 8, 12, 20, 25. The middle two are 8 and 12, so the median is $(8 + 12) \div 2 = 10$.

**Answer: (B) 10**
