The largest possible circle is drawn inside a square with sides of 10. What is the area of the part of the square outside the circle?

## Solution

The square has an area of 100. The circle has a radius of 5, so its area is $25\pi$. Subtract: $100 - 25\pi$.

**Answer: (D) $100 - 25\pi$**
