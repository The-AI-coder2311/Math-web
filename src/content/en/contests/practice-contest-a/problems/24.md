A bag has 5 red and 5 blue marbles. Two are drawn without putting the first one back. What is the probability that they are different colors?

## Solution

Whatever the first marble is, 5 of the 9 marbles left are the other color. So the probability is $\frac{5}{9}$.

**Answer: (E) $\frac{5}{9}$**
