Three fair coins are flipped. What is the probability of getting at least 2 heads?

## Solution

The outcomes with at least 2 heads are HHH, HHT, HTH, and THH. That's 4 out of 8, so the probability is $\frac{1}{2}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{1}{2}$**
