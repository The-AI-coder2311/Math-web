How many whole numbers from 1 to 100 are divisible by 4 or by 6 (or both)?

## Solution

There are 25 multiples of 4 and 16 multiples of 6. Multiples of 12, of which there are 8, got counted twice. So $25 + 16 - 8 = 33$.

**Answer: (A) 33**
