Ava is 4 times as old as Ben. In 8 years, Ava will be twice as old as Ben. How old is Ava now?

## Solution

Let Ben be $b$. Then $4b + 8 = 2(b + 8)$, which gives $4b + 8 = 2b + 16$, so $b = 4$. Ava is 16.

**Answer: (E) 16**
