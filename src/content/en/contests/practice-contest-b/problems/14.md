A car travels at 40 miles per hour for 3 hours, and then at 60 miles per hour for 1 hour. What is its average speed for the whole trip, in miles per hour?

## Solution

The distance is $120 + 60 = 180$ miles in 4 hours, and $180 \div 4 = 45$.

**Answer: (A) 45**
