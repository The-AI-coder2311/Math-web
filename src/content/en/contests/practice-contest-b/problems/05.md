What is $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6}$?

## Solution

Use sixths: $\frac{2}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Answer: (E) $\frac{1}{2}$**
