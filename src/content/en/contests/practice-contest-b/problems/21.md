The table shows how many books 10 students read.

| Books | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Students | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 |

What is the median number of books?

## Solution

In order, the numbers are 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4. The 5th and 6th are both 2, so the median is 2.

**Answer: (C) 2**
