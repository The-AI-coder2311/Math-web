A slice of a circle with a radius of 8 has an angle of 45°. What is its area?

## Solution

The slice is $\frac{45}{360} = \frac{1}{8}$ of the circle. The whole area is $64\pi$, so the slice has an area of $8\pi$.

**Answer: (D) $8\pi$**
