A teacher has a box of between 50 and 80 pencils. She can share them equally among 6 students with none left over. She could also share them equally among 8 students with none left over. How many pencils are in the box?

## Solution

The number has to be a multiple of both 6 and 8. List the multiples of 8 and look for the first one that 6 also divides: 8, 16, 24. So the numbers that work are the multiples of 24: 24, 48, 72, 96, and so on.

Only 72 is between 50 and 80. Check: $72 \div 6 = 12$ and $72 \div 8 = 9$.

If you picked 48, it works for 6 and 8, but it's less than 50.

**Answer: (D) 72**
