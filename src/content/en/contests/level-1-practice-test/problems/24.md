A drawer holds 6 red socks, 8 blue socks, and 10 black socks. The room is dark, so you can't see colors. What is the smallest number of socks you must take out to be sure you have 3 socks of the same color?

## Solution

Imagine the unluckiest possible draws. You could take 2 red, 2 blue, and 2 black, which is 6 socks with no 3 of the same color. So 6 socks aren't enough.

Now take one more sock. Whatever color it is, that color now has 3 socks. So 7 socks always work.

**Answer: (D) 7**
