A bag holds 3 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. You pull out one marble without looking. What is the probability that it is blue?

## Solution

There are $3 + 5 + 2 = 10$ marbles, and 5 of them are blue. The probability is $\frac{5}{10}$, which simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$.

If you got $\frac{5}{8}$, you left out the green marbles. Every marble in the bag counts toward the total.

**Answer: (A) $\frac{1}{2}$**
