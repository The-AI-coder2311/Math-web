How many positive whole numbers divide evenly into 36?

## Solution

List the factor pairs, starting from 1:

$1 \times 36$, $2 \times 18$, $3 \times 12$, $4 \times 9$, and $6 \times 6$.

After 6, the pairs start repeating, so you can stop. That gives 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, and 36. The pair $6 \times 6$ adds only one factor, so there are 9 factors, not 10.

**Answer: (C) 9**
