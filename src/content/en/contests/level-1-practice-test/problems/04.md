A rectangle is 9 inches long and 4 inches wide. What is its perimeter, in inches?

## Solution

Perimeter is the distance all the way around. A rectangle has two long sides and two short sides: $9 + 4 + 9 + 4 = 26$.

Watch out for 36. That's the area, $9 \times 4$, which measures the space inside.

**Answer: (C) 26**
