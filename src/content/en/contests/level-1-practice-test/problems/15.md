Six people are at a meeting. Each person shakes hands exactly once with every other person. How many handshakes are there in all?

## Solution

Each of the 6 people shakes 5 hands, which gives $6 \times 5 = 30$. But every handshake involves 2 people, so it was counted twice, once for each person. Divide by 2: $30 \div 2 = 15$.

**Answer: (D) 15**
