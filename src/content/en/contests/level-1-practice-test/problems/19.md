A fair coin is flipped 4 times. What is the probability of getting at least one head?

## Solution

"At least one" has many cases, so count the opposite instead. The only way to get no heads is tails all 4 times. There are $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 16$ outcomes, and only 1 of them is all tails. So the probability of no heads is $\frac{1}{16}$, and the probability of at least one head is $1 - \frac{1}{16} = \frac{15}{16}$.

**Answer: (E) $\frac{15}{16}$**
