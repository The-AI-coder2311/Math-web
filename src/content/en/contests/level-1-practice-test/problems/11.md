Maya thinks of a number. She doubles it, subtracts 6, and then divides by 4. Her final answer is 5. What number did she start with?

## Solution

Start at the end and undo each step in reverse order.

- She divided by 4 to get 5, so before that she had $5 \times 4 = 20$.
- She subtracted 6 to get 20, so before that she had $20 + 6 = 26$.
- She doubled to get 26, so she started with $26 \div 2 = 13$.

Check it forward: $13 \times 2 = 26$, then $26 - 6 = 20$, then $20 \div 4 = 5$.

**Answer: (A) 13**
