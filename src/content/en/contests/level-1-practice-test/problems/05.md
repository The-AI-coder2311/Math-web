Beads are strung on a necklace in this repeating order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and so on. What color is the 53rd bead?

## Solution

The pattern repeats every 5 beads, so beads 5, 10, 15, and so on up to 50 are all blue. After bead 50 the pattern starts over: bead 51 is red, bead 52 is orange, and bead 53 is yellow.

Another way to see it: $53 = 5 \times 10 + 3$, so the 53rd bead matches the 3rd bead.

**Answer: (B) Yellow**
