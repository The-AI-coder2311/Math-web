A café sells 4 kinds of sandwiches and 3 kinds of soup. A lunch special is one sandwich and one soup. How many different lunch specials are possible?

## Solution

Each of the 4 sandwiches can go with each of the 3 soups. One choice and then another means multiply: $4 \times 3 = 12$.

If you picked 7, you added. Adding counts the sandwiches and soups, not the pairs.

**Answer: (A) 12**
