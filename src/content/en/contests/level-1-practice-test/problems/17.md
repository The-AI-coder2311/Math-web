A jacket costs 60 dollars. The store raises its price by 20%. Later, the store lowers the new price by 20%. What is the final price, in dollars?

## Solution

The price goes up by 20% of 60, which is $0.2 \times 60 = 12$. The new price is $60 + 12 = 72$ dollars.

Then it goes down by 20% of 72, which is $0.2 \times 72 = 14.4$. The final price is $72 - 14.4 = 57.6$ dollars, or 57.60 dollars.

It doesn't come back to 60, because the second 20% is taken from a bigger number.

**Answer: (B) 57.60**
