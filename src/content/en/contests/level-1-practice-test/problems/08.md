At an animal shelter, the ratio of cats to dogs is $3 : 5$. There are 40 cats and dogs in all. How many dogs are there?

## Solution

Draw 3 boxes for cats and 5 boxes for dogs. That's 8 equal boxes holding 40 animals, so each box holds $40 \div 8 = 5$ animals. The dogs fill 5 boxes: $5 \times 5 = 25$.

Check: there are $3 \times 5 = 15$ cats, and $15 + 25 = 40$.

**Answer: (D) 25**
