Two ordinary six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the two numbers add up to 7?

## Solution

Think of the dice as a red die and a blue die. Each has 6 results, so there are $6 \times 6 = 36$ equally likely outcomes. The pairs that add to 7 are (1, 6), (2, 5), (3, 4), (4, 3), (5, 2), and (6, 1). That's 6 outcomes, so the probability is $\frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6}$.

A common trap is $\frac{1}{11}$. The 11 possible sums from 2 to 12 aren't equally likely, so you have to count the 36 real outcomes.

**Answer: (A) $\frac{1}{6}$**
