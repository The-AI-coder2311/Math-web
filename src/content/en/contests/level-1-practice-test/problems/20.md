How many three-digit numbers have at least one digit that is a 7?

## Solution

Count the numbers with no 7 at all, then subtract.

There are 900 three-digit numbers, from 100 to 999. For a number with no 7, the first digit can be any of 1 to 9 except 7, which is 8 choices. The second and third digits can be any of 0 to 9 except 7, which is 9 choices each. That's $8 \times 9 \times 9 = 648$ numbers with no 7.

So $900 - 648 = 252$ numbers have at least one 7.

**Answer: (A) 252**
