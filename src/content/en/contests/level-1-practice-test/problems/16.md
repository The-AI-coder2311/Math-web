One angle of a triangle measures 40 degrees. The other two angles are equal to each other. How many degrees is each of the other two angles?

## Solution

The angles of a triangle add up to 180 degrees. Take away the 40-degree angle: $180 - 40 = 140$. The two equal angles share those 140 degrees, so each one is $140 \div 2 = 70$ degrees.

Check: $40 + 70 + 70 = 180$.

**Answer: (E) 70**
