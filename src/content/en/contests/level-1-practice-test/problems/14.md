A printer prints 45 pages in 3 minutes. Printing at the same rate, how many pages does it print in 10 minutes?

## Solution

First find the pages for one minute: $45 \div 3 = 15$. In 10 minutes it prints $15 \times 10 = 150$ pages.

**Answer: (B) 150**
