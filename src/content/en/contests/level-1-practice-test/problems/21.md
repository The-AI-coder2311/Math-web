A rectangle is 10 inches long and 8 inches wide. A 3-inch by 3-inch square is cut out of each of its four corners, which leaves a shape like a plus sign. What is the perimeter of the shape that is left, in inches?

## Solution

Draw one corner. Before the cut, the corner had a 3-inch piece of the top edge and a 3-inch piece of the side edge. After the cut, those two pieces are gone, but the cut makes two new edges: one 3 inches long going down and one 3 inches long going across. The length you lose and the length you gain are the same.

That happens at all four corners, so the perimeter doesn't change. It's still the rectangle's perimeter: $10 + 8 + 10 + 8 = 36$.

The area does shrink, from 80 to $80 - 4 \times 9 = 44$ square inches. That's why 44 is a tempting wrong answer.

**Answer: (C) 36**
