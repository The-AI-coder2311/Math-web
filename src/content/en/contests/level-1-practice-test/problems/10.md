A triangle has a base of 10 centimeters and a height of 7 centimeters. What is its area, in square centimeters?

## Solution

A triangle is half of a rectangle with the same base and height. The rectangle would have area $10 \times 7 = 70$, so the triangle has area $70 \div 2 = 35$.

If you picked 70, you forgot to take half.

**Answer: (B) 35**
