What is the value of $\left(1 - \frac{1}{4}\right)\left(1 - \frac{1}{9}\right)\left(1 - \frac{1}{16}\right) \cdots \left(1 - \frac{1}{144}\right)$?

## Solution

Each piece is a difference of squares, such as $\left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{2}\right)$. The pieces with a minus telescope to $\frac{1}{12}$, and the pieces with a plus telescope to $\frac{13}{2}$. The product is $\frac{13}{24}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{13}{24}$**
