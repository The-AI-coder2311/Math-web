What is the value of $3 + 7 + 11 + \cdots + 79$?

## Solution

The climb is 76 in steps of 4, so there are 19 steps and 20 terms. The sum is $\frac{20 \times (3 + 79)}{2} = 820$.

**Answer: (D) 820**
