Points $A$, $B$, and $C$ are on a circle with center $O$, with $C$ on the longer arc from $A$ to $B$. Angle $OAB$ measures 20°. How many degrees does angle $ACB$ measure?

## Solution

Triangle $OAB$ is isosceles, so angle $AOB$ is $180 - 20 - 20 = 140$ degrees. Angle $ACB$ is half of that: 70°.

**Answer: (B) 70**
