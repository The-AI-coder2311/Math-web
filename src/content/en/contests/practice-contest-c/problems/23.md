The sum $6 + 2 + \frac{2}{3} + \frac{2}{9} + \cdots$ goes on forever. What number does it equal?

## Solution

The first term is 6, and the ratio is $\frac{1}{3}$. The sum is $6 \div \frac{2}{3} = 9$.

**Answer: (B) 9**
