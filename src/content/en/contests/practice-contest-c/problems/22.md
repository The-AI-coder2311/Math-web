Let $r$ and $s$ be the solutions of $x^2 - 7x + 4 = 0$. What is $r^2 + s^2$?

## Solution

$r + s = 7$ and $rs = 4$, so $r^2 + s^2 = 49 - 8 = 41$.

**Answer: (A) 41**
