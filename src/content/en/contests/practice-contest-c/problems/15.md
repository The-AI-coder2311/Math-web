A circle has a radius of 8. Point $P$ is 17 units from the center. How long is the tangent from $P$ to the circle?

## Solution

The radius meets the tangent at a right angle, and 17 is the hypotenuse. So the tangent is $\sqrt{289 - 64} = 15$.

**Answer: (B) 15**
