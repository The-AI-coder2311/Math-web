A line passes through $(1, 3)$ and $(3, 7)$. Where does it cross the $y$-axis? Give the $y$-coordinate.

## Solution

The slope is $\frac{7 - 3}{3 - 1} = 2$. So $3 = 2 \times 1 + b$, which gives $b = 1$.

**Answer: (C) 1**
