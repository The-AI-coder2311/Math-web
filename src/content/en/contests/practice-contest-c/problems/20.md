Chords $AB$ and $CD$ of a circle cross at $E$. If $AE = 5$, $EB = 6$, and $CE = 3$, how long is $ED$?

## Solution

The products of the pieces are equal: $5 \times 6 = 3 \times ED$. So $ED = 10$.

**Answer: (B) 10**
