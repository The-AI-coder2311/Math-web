How many points lie on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 65$ if both coordinates are integers? (Integers are whole numbers and their negatives, so $(-1, 8)$ would count.)

## Solution

The pairs of squares are $1 + 64$ and $16 + 49$. Each gives 8 points, using both orders and both signs, so the total is 16.

**Answer: (E) 16**
