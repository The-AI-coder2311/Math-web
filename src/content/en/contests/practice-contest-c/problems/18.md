What is the area of the triangle with corners $(0, 0)$, $(7, 1)$, and $(2, 6)$?

## Solution

Using the shoelace method: $\frac{7 \times 6 - 2 \times 1}{2} = \frac{40}{2} = 20$.

**Answer: (A) 20**
