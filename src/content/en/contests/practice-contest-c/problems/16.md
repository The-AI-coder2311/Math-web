Positive whole numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy $xy + x + y = 34$. What is $x + y$?

## Solution

Add 1 to get $(x + 1)(y + 1) = 35$. Both factors are at least 2, so they're 5 and 7. So $x$ and $y$ are 4 and 6, and $x + y = 10$.

**Answer: (B) 10**
