A class of 20 students has a mean score of 80, and a class of 30 students has a mean score of 70. What is the mean score of all 50 students?

## Solution

The total is $20 \times 80 + 30 \times 70 = 3700$, and $3700 \div 50 = 74$.

**Answer: (B) 74**
