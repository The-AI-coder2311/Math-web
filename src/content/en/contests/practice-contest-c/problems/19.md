Three standard dice are rolled. What is the probability that the numbers add up to 10?

## Solution

Count by the first die. If it shows 1 through 6, the other two dice have to add up to 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, or 4, which gives 4, 5, 6, 5, 4, and 3 outcomes. That's 27 out of 216, so the probability is $\frac{27}{216} = \frac{1}{8}$.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{1}{8}$**
