What is the smallest positive whole number that leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 3, a remainder of 3 when divided by 4, and a remainder of 4 when divided by 5?

## Solution

The number is 1 short of a multiple of 3, 4, and 5. The LCM is 60, so the number is 59.

**Answer: (E) 59**
