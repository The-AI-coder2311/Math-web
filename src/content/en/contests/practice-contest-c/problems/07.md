What is the remainder when $7^{100}$ is divided by 5?

## Solution

$7 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, and $2^4 = 16 \equiv 1$. Since 100 is a multiple of 4, the remainder is 1.

**Answer: (C) 1**
