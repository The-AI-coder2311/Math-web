A bag has 2 red, 3 blue, and 5 green marbles. One is picked at random. What is the probability that it is blue?

## Solution

There are 10 marbles, and 3 are blue, so the probability is $\frac{3}{10}$.

**Answer: (B) $\frac{3}{10}$**
