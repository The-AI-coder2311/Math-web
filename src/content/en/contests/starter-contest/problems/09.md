A recipe uses 3 cups of rice for 4 people. How many cups are needed for 10 people?

## Solution

Each person needs $3 \div 4 = 0.75$ cups. So 10 people need $10 \times 0.75 = 7.5$ cups.

**Answer: (A) 7.5**
