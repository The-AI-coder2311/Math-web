A rectangle that is 10 by 8 has a 4 by 3 rectangle cut out of one corner. What is the area of the shape that remains?

## Solution

Subtract the part you don't want: $10 \times 8 - 4 \times 3 = 80 - 12 = 68$.

**Answer: (C) 68**
