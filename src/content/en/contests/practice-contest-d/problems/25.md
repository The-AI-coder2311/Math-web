How many ordered pairs of positive whole numbers $(x, y)$ satisfy $\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = \frac{1}{6}$?

## Solution

Multiply both sides by $6xy$ to get $6y + 6x = xy$. Move everything to one side and add 36 to both sides: $xy - 6x - 6y + 36 = 36$, which factors as $(x - 6)(y - 6) = 36$. Both $x$ and $y$ are greater than 6, because each fraction is smaller than $\frac{1}{6}$. So $x - 6$ can be any positive factor of 36. Since $36 = 2^2 \times 3^2$, it has $3 \times 3 = 9$ factors, and each one gives one pair.

**Answer: (E) 9**
