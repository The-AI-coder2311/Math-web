What is the remainder when $2^{50}$ is divided by 7?

## Solution

$2^3 = 8$, which leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 7. Since $50 = 3 \times 16 + 2$, we can write $2^{50} = (2^3)^{16} \times 2^2$. That leaves the same remainder as $1 \times 4 = 4$.

**Answer: (E) 4**
