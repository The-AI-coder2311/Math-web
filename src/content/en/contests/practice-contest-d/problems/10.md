Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the product of the two numbers is odd?

## Solution

A product is odd only when both numbers are odd. Each die shows an odd number with probability $\frac{1}{2}$, so the probability is $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{1}{4}$**
