The price of a jacket is raised by 25%. Then the new price is lowered by 20%. The final price is what percent of the original price?

## Solution

Raising by 25% multiplies the price by 1.25, and lowering by 20% multiplies it by 0.8. Since $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$, the final price equals the original price. That's 100% of it.

**Answer: (B) 100**
