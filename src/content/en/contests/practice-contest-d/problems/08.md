In an arithmetic sequence, the 5th term is 23 and the 12th term is 58. What is the 20th term?

## Solution

From the 5th term to the 12th term is 7 steps, and the terms go up by $58 - 23 = 35$. So each step adds 5. The 20th term is 8 steps past the 12th term: $58 + 8 \times 5 = 98$.

**Answer: (E) 98**
