How many different ways can the letters of the word LEVEL be arranged?

## Solution

Five different letters could be arranged in 120 ways. But LEVEL has two Ls and two Es, and swapping matching letters doesn't make a new arrangement. Divide by $2 \times 2 = 4$ to get $120 \div 4 = 30$.

**Answer: (B) 30**
