How many 3-digit numbers have three different digits that are all odd?

## Solution

The odd digits are 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9. The first digit has 5 options, the second has 4, and the third has 3. That's $5 \times 4 \times 3 = 60$ numbers.

**Answer: (D) 60**
