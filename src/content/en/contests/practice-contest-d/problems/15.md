A chord of a circle has a length of 24, and it is 5 units from the center of the circle. What is the radius of the circle?

## Solution

The line from the center to the middle of the chord meets the chord at a right angle. That makes a right triangle with legs of 5 and 12, and the radius is its hypotenuse. Since $5^2 + 12^2 = 169$, the radius is 13.

**Answer: (C) 13**
