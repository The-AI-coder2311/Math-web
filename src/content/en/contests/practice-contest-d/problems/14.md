The lines $y = x$ and $y = -x + 8$ and the $x$-axis form a triangle. What is its area?

## Solution

The two lines cross where $x = -x + 8$, which is at the point $(4, 4)$. They meet the $x$-axis at $(0, 0)$ and $(8, 0)$. The triangle has a base of 8 and a height of 4, so its area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 4 = 16$.

**Answer: (A) 16**
