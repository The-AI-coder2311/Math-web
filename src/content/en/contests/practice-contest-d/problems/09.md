An isosceles triangle has two sides of length 13 and a base of length 24. What is its area?

## Solution

Draw the height to the base. It splits the base into two pieces of 12, and each half of the triangle is a right triangle with a hypotenuse of 13. That's a 5-12-13 triangle, so the height is 5. The area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 24 \times 5 = 60$.

**Answer: (A) 60**
