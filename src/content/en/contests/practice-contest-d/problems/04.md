A rectangle has a perimeter of 30, and its length is twice its width. What is its area?

## Solution

The length and the width add up to half of the perimeter, which is 15. The length is twice the width, so the width is 5 and the length is 10. The area is $5 \times 10 = 50$.

**Answer: (C) 50**
