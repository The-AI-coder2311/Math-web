The equation $x^2 - 10x + 18 = 0$ has two solutions, $r$ and $s$. What is the value of $\frac{1}{r} + \frac{1}{s}$?

## Solution

Combine the two fractions: $\frac{1}{r} + \frac{1}{s} = \frac{r + s}{rs}$. For this equation, the solutions add up to 10 and multiply to 18. So the value is $\frac{10}{18} = \frac{5}{9}$.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{5}{9}$**
