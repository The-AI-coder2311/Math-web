The mean of 5 numbers is 12. When a sixth number is added to the list, the mean becomes 13. What is the sixth number?

## Solution

Five numbers with a mean of 12 add up to $5 \times 12 = 60$. Six numbers with a mean of 13 add up to $6 \times 13 = 78$. The sixth number is $78 - 60 = 18$.

**Answer: (D) 18**
