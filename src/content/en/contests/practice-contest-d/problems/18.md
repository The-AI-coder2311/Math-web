What is the smallest positive whole number that leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 5, and a remainder of 4 when divided by 7?

## Solution

List the numbers that leave a remainder of 4 when divided by 7: 4, 11, 18, 25, and so on. Check each one's remainder when divided by 5. The number 4 leaves 4, the number 11 leaves 1, and the number 18 leaves 3. So the answer is 18.

**Answer: (B) 18**
