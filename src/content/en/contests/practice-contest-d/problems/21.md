Rosa walks from home to school on a grid of streets. The school is 4 blocks east and 4 blocks north of her home, and she only walks east or north. The corner that is 2 blocks east and 2 blocks north of her home is closed, so she can't pass through it. How many different routes can Rosa take?

## Solution

With no closed corner, a route is 4 blocks east and 4 blocks north in some order. There are 70 ways to choose which 4 of the 8 blocks go east. Now count the routes through the closed corner. There are 6 routes from home to that corner, and 6 routes from that corner to school, so $6 \times 6 = 36$ routes pass through it. That leaves $70 - 36 = 34$ routes.

**Answer: (B) 34**
