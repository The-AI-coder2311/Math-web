What is the value of $\frac{1}{1 \times 3} + \frac{1}{3 \times 5} + \frac{1}{5 \times 7} + \cdots + \frac{1}{19 \times 21}$?

## Solution

Each fraction splits into two pieces. For example, $\frac{1}{1 \times 3} = \frac{1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right)$, and the others work the same way. When you add them all, the middle pieces cancel. What's left is $\frac{1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{21}\right) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{20}{21} = \frac{10}{21}$.

**Answer: (D) $\frac{10}{21}$**
