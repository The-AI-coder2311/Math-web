How many of the positive factors of 144 are perfect squares?

## Solution

$144 = 2^4 \times 3^2$. A square factor uses an even number of each prime. It can have 0, 2, or 4 twos, and 0 or 2 threes. That's $3 \times 2 = 6$ factors: 1, 4, 9, 16, 36, and 144.

**Answer: (B) 6**
