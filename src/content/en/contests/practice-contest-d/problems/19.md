Three fair coins are flipped, and you are told that at least one of them landed on heads. What is the probability that all three landed on heads?

## Solution

Three coins have 8 equally likely outcomes. The clue rules out only one of them, TTT, which leaves 7. One of those 7 is HHH, so the probability is $\frac{1}{7}$.

**Answer: (C) $\frac{1}{7}$**
