In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ is on side $AB$ and point $E$ is on side $AC$, and $DE$ is parallel to $BC$. If $AD = 4$, $DB = 6$, and the area of triangle $ABC$ is 75, what is the area of the four-sided region $DBCE$?

## Solution

Triangle $ADE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$, and its sides are $\frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5}$ as long. Areas scale by the square of that, which is $\frac{4}{25}$. So triangle $ADE$ has an area of $75 \times \frac{4}{25} = 12$, and the region $DBCE$ is the rest: $75 - 12 = 63$.

**Answer: (E) 63**
