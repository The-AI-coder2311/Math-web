A triangle has sides of length 13, 14, and 15. What is the radius of the circle inside the triangle that touches all three sides?

## Solution

Half of the perimeter is 21. Heron's formula gives an area of $\sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = \sqrt{7056} = 84$. The area also equals the radius times half of the perimeter, so the radius is $84 \div 21 = 4$.

**Answer: (A) 4**
