In a geometric sequence of positive numbers, the 2nd term is 6 and the 4th term is 54. What is the sum of the first 5 terms?

## Solution

Going from the 2nd term to the 4th term multiplies by the ratio twice. Since $54 \div 6 = 9$, the ratio is 3, and the 1st term is $6 \div 3 = 2$. The first five terms are 2, 6, 18, 54, and 162, and $2 + 6 + 18 + 54 + 162 = 242$.

**Answer: (D) 242**
