In how many ways can 8 identical stickers be given to 3 children, if each child gets at least 1 sticker?

## Solution

Give each child 1 sticker first. That leaves 5 stickers to share with no rule. With stars and bars, line up 5 stars and 2 bars, and choose which 2 of the 7 spots hold the bars. That's $\frac{7 \times 6}{2} = 21$ ways.

**Answer: (A) 21**
