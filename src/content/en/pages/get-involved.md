{{siteName}} is built by volunteers. There are three ways to help, and none of them need you to be a math star. Everything happens by email, so there are no forms and no accounts. The buttons at the bottom of this page open an email to us with the right subject line.

## Volunteer

We need people who enjoy explaining things kindly. You could:

- **Check problems.** Solve our practice problems before students see them, and tell us when wording is confusing or an answer looks wrong. This is the most helpful job of all.
- **Write lessons and problems.** Follow our writing rules: explain every step for a student who has never competed, and make every problem original.
- **Translate.** Help us bring lessons and problems into Spanish and other languages.
- **Test the site.** Try it on a school Chromebook, an older phone, or with a screen reader, and tell us what doesn't work.

Tell us a little about yourself, which job sounds fun, and about how many hours a month you'd like to give.

## Submit an original problem

We love new problems, especially ones that teach an idea in a surprising way. Please send:

1. **The problem**, with five answer choices if it's multiple choice.
2. **The answer and a full solution** that a 6th grader could follow.
3. **Where the idea came from.** The problem must be your own work. Please don't send real contest problems from the AMC, MATHCOUNTS, or a textbook, even with the numbers changed. They belong to the people who wrote them.

**What happens next:**

1. **We check that it's original.** We compare it with past official problems, such as the ones collected on the AoPS wiki.
2. **Two volunteers solve it on their own,** without seeing your answer. If they don't both get your answer, we talk it through with you.
3. **We fit it into a unit.** We add hints, a note for each wrong answer choice, and a short piece of code that checks the answer by computer.
4. **We tell you when it's live.** If you'd like, we'll thank you by first name on our About page.

Not every problem will make it onto the site, and we may edit the wording to match our style. We'll always tell you what we decided.

## Partner as a school

If your school or club uses {{siteName}}, we'd love to hear from you. Partner schools help us by:

- Telling us what works and what doesn't with real students.
- Letting us know which topics or languages their students need next.
- Sharing (anonymous) stories we can use to show other schools what's possible.

Partnering is free, and there's nothing to sign. We don't need any student names or information. You can also print our [recruiting kit](/teachers/recruiting-kit/) and [club plan](/teachers/club-plan/) to get started.
