## A letter to families

Date: ____________________

Dear families,

Our school is starting a **math club**, and your student is invited to join. I'm writing to tell you what the club is and how it works.

**What is it?** The club meets once a week for about 45 minutes. Students work in small groups on puzzles and problems that are a little different from regular math class. The goal is to build problem-solving skills and confidence, and to have fun doing it.

**Who can join?** Any interested student. There are no tryouts, and students don't need to be at the top of their math class. Students who like puzzles, games, or building things often love it.

**Is there a cost?** **No. The club is free to join.** Some students may choose to take part in an official math contest later in the year. That is always optional, and I will send home information ahead of time if your student is interested.

**What will students use?** We use paper, pencils, and a free website called {{siteName}}. The website has no ads and no accounts, and it doesn't collect any information about your student. Progress is saved only on the device your student uses.

**How can I help at home?** Ask your student to show you a problem they found interesting, and ask how they solved it. It's fine if neither of you knows the answer right away. Getting stuck and trying again is a big part of what we practice.
