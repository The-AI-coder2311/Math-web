## Our mission

Math competitions open doors, but mostly for students who already know they exist. {{siteName}} helps students from underserved schools take their first step, and gives their teachers what they need to start a team.

## Why we built this

There are great resources for students who already compete, but most of them assume you've competed before. Paid prep classes aren't an option for everyone either. We wanted something for the very first day: free, friendly, and written for someone who has never seen a competition problem.

## Who we are

{{siteName}} is run by high school student volunteers who love math and want more students to get a chance to enjoy it.

## What we promise

- **Free.** There's nothing to pay and no account to create.
- **Private.** {{privacy}}
- **Original and carefully checked.** We write our own practice problems and check every answer. If you ever spot a mistake, please tell us, and we'll fix it.

## Volunteer with us

We need people to write lessons, check problems, and translate the site. You don't need to be a math star. You only need to enjoy explaining things kindly. Our [Get Involved page](/get-involved/) explains how to volunteer, submit an original problem, or partner as a school.
