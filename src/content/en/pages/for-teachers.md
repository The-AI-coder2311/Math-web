{{siteName}} is a free website that teaches math competition skills from the very beginning, with lessons and practice for the AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS, and the AMC 10. It's built for students who have never competed, and for the teachers, coaches, and parents who want to help them.

**It's free, with no accounts.** Students don't sign up for anything, and their progress stays on their own device. You don't need a competition background either. Every problem comes with a full solution that you can read before you meet.
